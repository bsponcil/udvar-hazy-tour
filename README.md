# Udvar-Hazy Audio Tour — Support

Udvar-Hazy Audio Tour is a free, self-guided audio tour of the Smithsonian's Steven F. Udvar-Hazy Center in Chantilly, Virginia. Narrated by docent Brian Douglas, it tells the stories behind many of the museum's most significant aircraft and spacecraft, from the Space Shuttle Discovery and the SR-71 Blackbird to the Enola Gay and the Concorde.

## Features

- Audio narration for dozens of exhibits, with more added over time, plus photos and background reading for each
- Works completely offline — no Wi-Fi or cell signal needed in the museum
- Keeps playing with the screen locked, with controls on the Lock Screen
- Free, with no ads, accounts, or in-app purchases

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an internet connection?**
No. All narration and photos are built into the app. You only need a connection to open the optional reference links on exhibit pages.

**Can I listen with my phone in my pocket?**
Yes. Audio keeps playing when the screen is locked, and you can pause or resume from the Lock Screen or your headphones.

**What do I need to run the app?**
An iPhone running iOS 26.4 or later.

**Is this an official Smithsonian app?**
No. This is an independent audio tour. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Smithsonian Institution or the National Air and Space Museum. For museum hours, directions, and visitor information, visit [airandspace.si.edu](https://airandspace.si.edu/udvar-hazy-center).
