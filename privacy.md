# Privacy Policy — Udvar-Hazy Audio Tour

**Effective date:** November 24, 2026

Udvar-Hazy Audio Tour ("the app") is a free, independent audio tour of the Smithsonian's Steven F. Udvar-Hazy Center, created by Brian Douglas. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## The short version

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

## Details

- **No personal data.** The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information. There are no accounts or sign-ins.
- **No location tracking.** The app does not access your device's location.
- **No analytics, advertising, or tracking.** The app contains no analytics tools, advertising, or third-party tracking code, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
- **Works offline.** All audio narration and photos are built into the app. The app does not send any information over the internet.
- **Links to other websites.** Some exhibit pages include links to outside websites, such as Wikipedia. These open in your web browser and are governed by those websites' own privacy policies.
- **Apple.** If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports. This is controlled by you and handled under Apple's privacy policy.

## Children's privacy

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
